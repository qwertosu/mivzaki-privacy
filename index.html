Introduction

The "Mivzaki" application (hereinafter: "the App") respects user privacy. This privacy policy explains what data is collected, how it is stored, and how it is used.

Information Collected

Usage data: The App uses Firebase Analytics to collect usage data such as: screen views, searches, source filtering, favorites usage, settings, and the number of followed topics. This data is linked to an app instance identifier and does not include personally identifying information such as your name or email.

Crash data: The App uses Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports and technical device information in order to improve app stability.

Advertising: The App displays advertisements through Google AdMob. Google may collect and use device identifiers, such as the advertising ID, and your IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate location, in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

Notifications: The App uses Firebase Cloud Messaging to send notifications, such as the daily recap. This requires a device token that is managed by Google.

Technical data: To load content, the App connects to news websites and to our own server (for example, for the daily recap and its audio). Like any server, these may receive technical data such as your IP address. We do not use this data to identify users.

Local preferences: Settings such as theme, favorites, followed topics, and notification preferences are stored locally on the device only and are not sent to any server.

Information Not Collected

The App does not collect: name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or any other personally identifiable information.

Data Storage

All preferences, favorites, and followed topics are stored locally on the device. Usage and crash data are stored on Google's Firebase servers in accordance with Firebase's Terms of Service.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following services, each subject to its own privacy policy:

  • Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Cloud Messaging, Remote Config)
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Maps
  • Google Play services (in-app review and in-app updates)

Use of the App is also subject to our Terms of Service.
